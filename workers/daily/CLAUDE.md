# workers/daily/ — fixed training window and UTC days

- The sliding-window size is `training_window_days` in `shared/shared/config.py` (default 21, override with the `TRAINING_WINDOW_DAYS` environment variable) and is stored in `models.params["window_days"]`.
- The daily trainer uses every stored `BTCUSDT` daily row and fail with the required and available row counts when there are fewer than `(window + 1) * 5`: `required_training_days()` in `workers/daily/trainer.py`.
- The daily trainer and the predictor call `require_fresh_series(series.dates, today)` (`shared/shared/features.py`) before building features. It needs the last bar dated `today - 1` (UTC) and consecutive days, otherwise it raises `ValueError` naming the missing dates: the job exits 1, saves no prediction row and creates no model row (#174). `workers.daily.__main__` stops at the first failing stage, so the exit code reaches Railway. The workers never fetch prices themselves: `fetch-price` backfills missed days on its next run. The backtest (`scripts/backtest_engine.py`) has its own loader and does not call this check.
- "Today" is `shared.utils.utc_today()` in every job (predictor, evaluator and trainer, the backtest cron). `date.today()` follows the container's `TZ` (`America/Mexico_City` in `docker-compose.yml`), which is a day behind UTC from 00:00 to 06:00 UTC (#173).
- To test a job at that hour, use the `mexico_city_at_0300_utc` fixture of the root `conftest.py`. It sets `TZ=America/Mexico_City` and freezes `utc_today` at 2026-10-04 03:00 UTC, so a job that still calls `date.today()` fails. The price bars of such a test must end on the day before the frozen date (2026-10-03) and have no gaps, or `require_fresh_series` rejects them. Examples: `workers/daily/tests/test_utc_dates.py`, `workers/daily/tests/test_stale_series.py`.
